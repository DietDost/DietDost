      Your estimated targets

      Personalized starting point

      These estimates are for general adult use and may not fit pregnancy, medical conditions, or clinician-prescribed diets. Consult a qualified professional if needed.

      How are targets estimated?

      Calories use an adult resting-energy equation, then an activity multiplier and a small goal adjustment. Protein, carbohydrate, fat and fiber targets are practical estimates—not individualized medical prescriptions. Micronutrient reference values vary by age, sex and life stage; the app does not diagnose deficiencies.
